## Privacy

I self host this website at the Australian-based provider [Binary
Lane](https://binarylane.com.au), so I have access to the server logs which
shows your connecting IP address and user agent. This data is encrypted at rest,
though I doubt this has any value as personally identifiable information. My
only use for this information is to enforce IP-based `Fail2Ban` and `recidive`
bans (a futile attempt at deterring script-kiddies).

I can't see where you've come from, or where you're going, nor do I care.

In theory, I could measure your time on site, but I certainly don't care about
such analytics.
